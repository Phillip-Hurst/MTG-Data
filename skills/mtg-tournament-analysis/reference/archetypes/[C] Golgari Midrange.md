# Golgari Midrange

> [!warning] Banned 2026-08-10
> **Badgermole Cub** is banned in Standard.
>

>

**Casters: "Notorious deck — going to be 45-55% against the whole field." Returned to relevance via Professor Dellian Fel (SOS). Never truly runs out of steam. Attacks Izzet Prowess on the graveyard axis.**

7 players at PT Secrets of Strixhaven — Team The Coalition (Jason Reid, Matt Xu, Kenny Oswald, Kye Nelson, Brendan Hsu, Curtis Lam, Elad Stettner).

---

## Game plan

Grind. Professor Dellian Fel generates card advantage and handles threats. Keen-Eyed Curator attacks graveyards and scales into a large threat. Unholy Annex // Ritual Chamber and Firdoch Core drain the opponent while you develop. Strategic Betrayal is the metagame tech.

---

## Play patterns

- **Professor Dellian Fel** (SOS): 4 mana, 5 loyalty. Gains life vs aggro, draws cards vs control, destroys creatures in midrange mirrors. Magic.gg: "reminiscent of Jace, the Mind Sculptor." Easy to ultimate and establishes a steady clock.
- **Keen-Eyed Curator** (×3 main): eats 2 spells from opponent's graveyard on each attack. Grows with card types consumed — becomes a 7/7 trampler naturally. Also sideboard in vs Izzet Spell Elementals.
- **Unholy Annex // Ritual Chamber**: room card; Annex reliably draws cards while the Ritual Chamber side generates demons. Keeps hand full and produces 6/6 demons with Firdoch Core counting toward Demon threshold.
- **Firdoch Core** (×2): artifact that taps for mana and counts as a Demon. Life gain with Soulstone Sanctuary and Restless Cottage activates Dellian Fel's emblem faster.
- **Strategic Betrayal**: bounces a creature AND exiles the graveyard — shuts down Colorstorm Stallion + Boomerang Basics loop, denies Izzet Prowess recursion
- **Maelstrom Pulse**: hits multiple permanents with the same name — key vs Izzet Prowess's Otter tokens
- **Emeritus of Abundance** (×2, SOS): card advantage and selection
- **Llanowar Elves + Badgermole Cub**: mana acceleration for faster Dellian Fel

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Maelstrom Pulse on two Otters**: clears an Otter board in a single spell — key Izzet Prowess tech
- **Keen-Eyed Curator eating Colorstorm Stallion + Boomerang Basics**: both in yard = single activation covers the whole loop denial
- **Long Goodbye on Colorstorm Stallion**: deals with it cleanly without paying ward — cheaper than fighting through the trigger
- **Strategic Betrayal**: bounces Stallion to hand AND exiles graveyard in one activation. Ward becomes irrelevant.
- **Professor Dellian Fel ultimating fast**: 5 loyalty ticks to 7 in two turns; ultimate on turn 3 if unopposed
- **Unholy Annex // Ritual Chamber + Firdoch Core**: Annex draws and discards; Firdoch Core counts as a Demon for the Ritual Chamber's 6/6 demon production threshold

## Sideboard notes

**vs Izzet Prowess**:
- Bring in Strategic Betrayal — graveyard exile + remove the Stallion
- Bring in Long Goodbye — ward bypass on Stallion
- Keen-Eyed Curator (already main) neutralizes graveyard threats

**vs Izzet Lessons (adjacent)**:
- Maelstrom Pulse for Otter tokens

**vs Simic Omniscience**:
- Torpor Orb shuts off all ETB triggers — Omniscience can't bounce Marang River Regent to loop

**vs Sultai Control** (Kye Nelson beat Takeshi Ozawa in the feature match):
- Lean on Professor Dellian Fel — Sultai has no clean answer to planeswalkers short of Awaken the Honored Dead
- Play threats that require specific answers; Sultai's Withering Curse needs life gain first

---

## Weaknesses

- 40.3% win rate in melee data — actually performing below the "45-55%" expectation
- Slow to close games; opponents can stabilize and build out of Dellian Fel range
- Strategic Betrayal needs a creature target — graveyard threats without creatures dodge it
- Keen-Eyed Curator is slow vs fast decks that don't fill the yard
