# Jeskai Control

**Third-most represented at Portland RC. 12 players at PT Secrets of Strixhaven (3.7%), 44.2% win rate. Casters: "Tends to be an archetype that doesn't have a stock list — always a lot of frills and personal preferences."**

Key cards confirmed from Tarkir: Dragonstorm (TDM): Cori Mountain Monastery (land, creates monks), Jeskai Revelation (7-mana instant finisher), Marang River Regent (sideboard tech via Torpor Orb).

---

## Game plan

Sweep the board. Counter key spells. Close with Jeskai Revelation or a Tidebinder. Built specifically to beat creature-heavy strategies.

---

## Play patterns

- **Day of Judgment** (×4): the sweeper backbone — built to beat go-wide strategies like Simic Ouroboroid and Bant Airbending
- **Jeskai Revelation** at seven mana: take 4, draw 2, gain 4, make monks, bounce a permanent
- **Cori Mountain Monastery** (TDM #252): a Jeskai-colored land that creates 1/1 white Monk tokens with prowess when you cast spells at sorcery speed. Provides pressure (growing monk board) and selection. Casters consistently called it the card-advantage/pressure engine of the deck.
- **Stockup + Consult the Star Charts**: hit land drops, sculpt hand
- **No More Lies**: soft counterspell — irrelevant once opponent has enough mana (eventually outpaced)
- **Seam Rip**: targeted exile for graveyards; removes tokens; can be used twice on same creature to pump monks
- **Meticulous Archive**: surveil and filter draws
- **Get Lost**: flexible removal; also answers Omniscience as an enchantment
- **Tashana's Tidebinder** (sideboard): flash threat that counters abilities — great against combo decks

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Jeskai Revelation at end of opponent's turn**: forces them to tap mana, then untap and develop freely
- **Jeskai Revelation bouncing a planet land**: sends Simic Omniscience's Evendo, Waking Haven back to hand — delays or resets the combo
- **Two Seam Rips on a single creature**: pump two monks, draw a card off Monastery
- **Cori Mountain Monastery + sorcery**: each sorcery-speed spell makes a monk; Monastery provides pressure without needing dedicated creature slots

---

## Sideboard notes

**vs Simic Omniscience (boarding in)**:
- Wani Tong Librarian — flash threat; threatens the combo
- Negate — clean answer to noncreature spells
- Tashana's Tidebinder — threat + ability counter
- Torpor Orb — Jiao had no answer to this; Marang River Regent can't bounce enchantments, which locks the combo out entirely

**Game 1 weakness vs Simic Omniscience**: lot of creature respect in the maindeck, but Jiao isn't going wide. Postboard is much better.

---

## Weaknesses

- Game one against Simic Omniscience is rough — too many sweepers, not enough countermagic main
- No More Lies becomes dead once opponents develop enough mana
- Seven-mana Jeskai Revelation is a slow win condition against fast combo
- Needs to draw sweepers before go-wide decks get too far ahead

## Weekend update — Champions Cup JP/KR + Spotlight Secrets London (2026-05-12)

Source: OptimusTomTV (1UfUmY93K_k). 44 pilots, 4.6% combined metagame share, 48.6% non-mirror win rate. Below the cut line for popularity-tier B.

**New tech worth noting**: **Tablet of Discovery** is in the maindeck. Lets the deck fire Jeskai Revelation a turn ahead of curve. Champions Cup pilots in particular went deep on this — Japanese metagame tends to skew toward better control pilots and the deck got a regional bump from that.

**Beating up**:
- vs Selesnya Ouroboroid (favored — confirmed via the 27.8% reverse Ouroboroid result)
- vs Bant Rhythm and other rhythm decks (favored)
- vs Four-Color Elementals: 51.9% (slight edge)

**Falling apart**:
- **vs Dimir Excruciator**: low win rate. Maindecks aren't carrying enough counter magic to fight through Cavern of Souls. The removal suite (Firebending Lesson, Thunder Magics, A-Braids) is built for cubs, not for Excruciator's namesake threat or Superior Spider-Man.
- vs Selesnya Landfall and Mono-Green Landfall: disfavored but not unwinnable. Earthbender's Ascension and Bossing Say are harder to remove than the rhythm/cub package.

**Four-Color Control is the better control deck this weekend** — 53.3% combined win rate vs Jeskai's 48.6%. The black splash for Inevitable Defeat is the difference: exile-based removal handles the enchantment threats Jeskai struggles with.
