# Mardu Discard

**Excellent Day 1 performance. Chris Kraul went 2-0 over Mike Sigrist's Izzet Prowess in the feature match. New Secrets of Strixhaven cards (Hardened Academic, Practiced Offense) gave the deck teeth.**

---

## Game plan

Discard permanents to grow Ironcliff Elf and trigger Monument drain. Recur Bloodghast off every land drop. Use Practiced Offense (from graveyard) for double strike burst damage out of nowhere.

---

## Play patterns

- **Ironcliff Elf**: one-drop that grows each time you discard a permanent. With Moonshadow (menace), becomes nearly unblockable and answers two removal spells before dying.
- **Hardened Academic** (Secrets of Strixhaven): gains a counter when it ETBs; gives ALL your creatures a counter whenever anything enters from graveyard. Combine with Bloodghast's repeated recursion = your whole board keeps growing.
- **Practiced Offense** (Secrets of Strixhaven): gives a creature double strike when cast. Has a flashback-like mechanic — can also be played from graveyard. Bloodghast + Practiced Offense from graveyard on a land drop = surprise double strike kill.
- **Bloodghast**: returns from graveyard whenever a land enters. Repeatedly triggers Hardened Academic. Combine with Monument discard.
- Cool But Rude: leveling creature. At Level 2, deals 2 damage whenever you discard. Each Ironcliff Elf discard = 2 damage + Ironcliff grows.
- **Strategic Betrayal**: exile graveyard + exile a creature. Casters: "a phenomenal card up against these Spellemental style draws."

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Land drop + Bloodghast returns + Hardened Academic trigger**: all creatures get a counter — happens naturally every turn once both are in play
- **Practiced Offense from graveyard**: land ETBs, Bloodghast returns, cast Practiced Offense off Bloodghast trigger for double strike — sequence happens all at once
- **Kulbuth Ruud Level 2 + Ironcliff Elf discarding permanents**: each discard = 2 damage + Ironcliff grows. Multiple discards per turn = fast clock
- **Ironcliff Elf + Moonshadow**: menace means opponent needs two blockers. With 4+ counters, Ironcliff is a 5+ power creature requiring two blockers that can each survive it.

---

## Weaknesses

- Relies on graveyard (Bloodghast recursion, Practiced Offense); graveyard hate is effective
- Strategic Betrayal from the opponent can exile the whole engine
- Needs to discard permanents to grow Ironcliff — hand can run low
- Sweepers (Day of Judgment, Slagstorm) clear the growing board before the kill

## Weekend update — Spotlight Secrets London (2026-05-12)

Source: OptimusTomTV (1UfUmY93K_k). 4 pilots, 14-4 best record, 56.9% combined win rate, 8th-highest WR of the weekend. Koen de Vos's runner-up Spotlight London list ran an Azorius Momo deck and is tracked there, not here — but the Mardu Discard pilots had a strong showing in their own right.

**Why Mardu is clicking over Rakdos/Grixis versions**:
- **Hardened Academic** as a secondary discard outlet, beyond Ironcliff Elf and Cool But Rude
- **Practiced Offense** (cast or discard, then flashback from graveyard) gives the deck a go-wide angle the Rakdos build doesn't have
- The white splash adds **Practiced Offense double strike** that plays nicely with Carnage and Ironcliff — counters stay on the creatures, the deck just clicks

**Notably absent**: the Mardu build is NOT running Flameweave Phoenix. Rakdos lists kept it for the "beat through anything" plan; Mardu trades that for the wider Practiced Offense token board.

Still on theme: Blood Gas, Moon Shadow, Tur's Light Shatter.
