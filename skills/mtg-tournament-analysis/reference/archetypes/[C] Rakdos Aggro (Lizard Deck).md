# Rakdos Aggro (Lizard Deck)

**Casters: "Despite having been legal longer, the lizard deck is still less of a known quantity." 39% win rate at World Championship when it first appeared — got targeted immediately. Now adapted.**

---

## Game plan

Play cheap efficient creatures with built-in scaling. Hired Claw grows with every attack and deals incremental damage. Burnout Bastronaut engines up to Speed 4 for double strike. Close with haste threats off Rockface Village.

---

## Play patterns

- **Hired Claw**: every attack grows it; triggers for one damage to opponent every attack — scales quickly
- **Burnout Bastronaut**: Speed counter mechanic — becomes double strike at Speed 4; Hired Claw activation grows Bastronaut before combat
- **Flame Kosh Gecko**: two-drop that works like Burning Tree Emissary — ruminates by tapping; fills the board fast
- **Rockface Village**: gives Hired Claw haste — threatens surprise attacks
- **Opera Love Song**: end-of-turn card selection; find a mountain and a spell
- **Ember Heart Challenger**: four-power creature that triggers Rockface Village haste on Hired Claw

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Hired Claw activation while Bastronaut is in play**: Bastronaut hits Speed 4 before attacks resolve, making it double strike during combat
- **Scales of Shale in combat**: saves the Gecko, kills the blocker, gains 4 life — three-for-one combat trick
- **Burst Lightning as a finisher**: kicks for 4 direct damage; combines with Scales of Shale for instant-speed combat blow-outs
- **Rockface Village + Ember Heart Challenger**: play Challenger (4-power), trigger Village to give Hired Claw haste, surprise attack for a lot of damage

## Weaknesses

- Shoot the Sheriff can't target Hired Claw (it's an outlaw) — but opponents know this; they'll use Bitter Triumph or Requiting Hex
- Aggressive deck that runs out of gas against midrange board stabilization
- Day of Black Sun clears the small creature base efficiently
- Vender Relentless is a reasonable blocker that locks down the aggro plan
