# Bant Airbending

> [!warning] Banned 2026-08-10
> **Badgermole Cub** is banned in Standard. In this deck it was: Grows a land each time it re-enters — makes the kill.
>
> Everything below is a record of how the deck played before the 2026-08-10 banned and restricted announcement. The list can't be registered as written, and the matchup numbers describe a field that no longer exists. Read it as history.
>
> Announcement: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/banned-and-restricted-august-10-2026


**Won a Spotlight Avatar. Jumped from 6 players at prior event to 62 players at Portland RC after that win. Casters noted: "Mediocre beatdown plan as a backup. Okay you answered my combo — I have three 2/2s and a dream."**

Attacks the metagame from a sorcery-speed angle. Good against Cub decks that also play at sorcery speed.

---

## Game plan

Assemble two Ang Swift Saviors and an infinite loop to make either an infinitely large land (via Badgermole Cub) or infinite 1/1 tokens (via Ang at the Crossroads flip). Win by attacking with that.

Fair backup: flash in Ang Swift Savior to block, pressure with Bramble Familiar + Fetch Quest, use Formidable Speaker to tutor threats.

---

## Play patterns

- **Ang Swift Savior** has a beginning-of-combat trigger: exile a creature you control until Ang leaves (like Oblivion Ring on your own creature)
- **Two Ang Swift Saviors**: each one exiles the other. Both re-enter. Both triggers fire again. If any other permanent is on the board, it loops infinitely
- Off the infinite loop: cast whatever you need from hand for free, find Terror of the Peaks for a win, or grow a land infinitely via Badgermole Cub
- **Ang at the Crossroads flip**: flipping it generates infinite 1/1 tokens during the loop
- **Formidable Speaker**: discard a land, find Ang Swift Savior or whichever combo piece is missing
- **Fair plan**: Bramble Familiar into Fetch Quest on curve, Ang Swift Savior as a flash blocker, close with flyers
- Operates mostly at sorcery speed — better against decks that also play slowly

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Two Ang Swift Saviors**: exile each other simultaneously, both re-enter triggering again — infinite if any other permanent exists
- **Airbend away your own creatures mid-combat**: re-enter for free, trigger ETBs again
- **Badgermole Cub + loop**: Cub animates a land each time it enters; loop two Angs with Cub in play = infinitely large land as the win
- **Ang Swift Savior at beginning of combat**: exile an opponent's creature until Ang leaves (acts like Oblivion Ring — temporary removal for attackers)
- **Formidable Speaker + land in hand**: discard the land, search for Ang or any creature combo piece

## Weaknesses

- Mediocre beatdown backup — three 2/2s don't close fast against any real board
- Instant-speed interaction on Ang Swift Savior breaks the loop
- Needs two Ang Swift Saviors — vulnerable to hand disruption before setup
- Weak to decks that interact at flash/instant speed; plays mostly at sorcery speed
