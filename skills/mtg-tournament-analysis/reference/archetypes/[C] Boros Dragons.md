# Boros Dragons

**Excellent Day 1 win rate at PT Secrets of Strixhaven. Casters: "This Boros Dragons deck is definitely really meant to be taking advantage of cub decks." Beat Izzet Prowess 2-0 in the feature match.**

---

## Game plan

Play cheap flyers and ramp them with Momo Friendly Flyer. Deploy Nova Hellkite and Magmatic Hellkite as top-end threats. Use Voice of Victory to lock opponents out of instant-speed interaction. Close with flying damage before opponents stabilize.

---

## Play patterns

- **Momo Friendly Flyer**: taps to reduce cost of your next flyer; also pumps each time a flying creature ETBs. Early ramp + threat density.
- **Nova Hellkite** (impending counters): impend it early, deploy as a 6/6 flyer with haste later. Warp it at instant speed to eat Elusive Otters before they get pump triggers.
- **Magmatic Hellkite**: fire-breathing top-end threat
- **Sarkhan**: early Dragon/planeswalker that generates tempo and board presence
- **Voice of Victory**: prevents opponents from casting spells on your turn. Against Izzet Prowess, this is a complete lockout while your dragons attack. Casters: "Voice of Victory just says I'm good thanks."
- **Clarion Conqueror**: noted as potentially siding out vs Izzet (shuts off treasure which Boros needs for its own plan)
- "Anti-rhythm deck" — designed to prey on slower synergy builds and Cub decks

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Warp Nova Hellkite at instant speed**: deploy it on opponent's end step to eat Elusive Otters before they receive pump triggers from Stormchaser's Talent
- **Voice of Victory on your attack step**: opponent can't cast instants (Get Out, Prismari Charm, combat tricks) while you swing with dragons
- **Momo Friendly Flyer + flyer ETB chain**: each new flying creature pumps Momo; with multiple flyers ETBing in sequence, Momo grows substantially

---

## Weaknesses

- Built for Cub decks — mediocre vs non-creature strategies or pure combo
- Clarion Conqueror can conflict with the deck's own treasure requirements
- Sweepers (Day of Judgment) clear the flying board cleanly
- Slower than Izzet Prowess at deploying threats but wins by locking out instant-speed interaction
