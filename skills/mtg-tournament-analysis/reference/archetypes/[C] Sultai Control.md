# Sultai Control

**Team Moriyama Japan — Ken Yukuhiro, Akira Shibata, Takeshi Ozawa, Shuhei Nakamura, and Atsuki Kihara. 5 players. 47.5% win rate at PT Secrets of Strixhaven.** Magic.gg: "If Izzet Maestro is a flashy lecture, this deck is a carefully researched thesis."

---

## Game plan

Control the game with a suite of cheap, flexible interaction. Stock the graveyard with Ancient Cornucopia, Rakshasa's Bargain, and Awaken the Honored Dead. Use Withering Curse + Ancient Cornucopia to sweep the board for 3 mana. Win with Emeritus of Ideation — a 4/4 with ward 2 that gives you Ancestral Recall on ETB, backed up by Superior Spider-Man to keep recurring it.

This is fair magic, not the reanimator combo version (Dimir Excruciator). Grinds with card advantage. Beats midrange historically better than aggro or combo.

---

## Play patterns

- **Ancient Cornucopia**: mana rock + life gain. Taps for any color. Gaining life enables Withering Curse. Also lets you cast Rakshasa's Bargain more easily with non-BUG mana.
- **Withering Curse** (3 mana): if you've gained life this turn, destroy all creatures. Pairs with Ancient Cornucopia for a repeatable 3-mana Wrath.
- **Rakshasa's Bargain**: scry 4, put best 2 into hand, rest into graveyard. Digs deep, stocks the yard for Superior Spider-Man.
- **Awaken the Honored Dead**: removes a threat and mills into the yard. Hitting Emeritus of Ideation off a mill then casting Superior Spider-Man gets you Ancestral Recall immediately.
- **Emeritus of Ideation** (main win condition): ward 2, sizable body, casts Ancestral Recall when it enters. Grinds opponents out of resources.
- **Superior Spider-Man**: brings a creature back from the graveyard as though cast — so Emeritus triggers again. Recurring Emeritus = recurring Ancestral Recall.
- **Deceit**: bounce a permanent + look at opponent's hand and take a card. Two modes active for 4 mana.
- **Quandrix Charm**: multi-mode; one mode counters a spell (the mode Ozawa held it for). Soft disruption.
- **Witherbloom Charm**: gain 5 life (enables Withering Curse) / other modes.
- Deck is described as "like Dimir Excruciator, but without the over-the-top combo — just fair magic."

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Withering Curse + Ancient Cornucopia**: tap Cornucopia for mana (gain 1 life), cast Withering Curse — destroy all creatures for 3 total mana. Repeatable if you keep Cornucopia.
- **Emeritus of Ideation ward 2**: if opponent can't pay 2 extra, their removal fizzles. Often just sticks on the board.
- **Rakshasa's Bargain on opponent's turn**: stock the graveyard, then untap and Superior Spider-Man something back.
- **Deceit two-mode sequence**: bounce their best permanent AND take a card from their hand in the same activation. Casters called it "brutal" to have the planeswalker bounced and the card taken.
- **Withering Curse with Witherbloom Charm**: if you can't get life from Cornucopia, Witherbloom Charm (gain 5 life mode) enables Curse the same turn.

## Matchup notes

**vs Golgari Midrange** (featured game, Ozawa lost to Kye Nelson):
Golgari has Professor Dellian Fel (hard to answer without dedicated enchantment/planeswalker removal), Unholy Annex // Ritual Chamber for card advantage, and a faster clock. Ozawa couldn't find Emeritus in time and ran out of resources. Kai drew 10 cards off his engines while Ozawa drew "nothing but air."

Key interaction: Golgari sideboard Keen-Eyed Curator eats graveyard cards, limiting Superior Spider-Man targets. Golgari also has no efficient answer to Emeritus of Ideation short of exile — Withering Curse can't help here since it's their creature.

**vs Izzet Prowess** (12 matches in data, ~50/50):
Withering Curse sweeps all the Otter tokens. Deceit bounces Stormchaser's Talent. Emeritus + Ancestral Recall outgrinds the cantrip engine. The race is whether Prowess kills before the Curse fires.

---

## Weaknesses

- Professor Dellian Fel is very hard to answer (needs Wistfulness or Awaken the Honored Dead)
- Drawing air while opponent grinds card advantage loses quickly
- 47.5% overall win rate — slightly below water, likely unfavored vs Landfall variants
- Graveyard hate (Strategic Betrayal, Soul-Guide Lantern) cuts off the Superior Spider-Man recursion loop
- Only 5 players at the PT — small sample, but the deck performed exactly at expectations
