# Izzet Lessons

> [!warning] Banned 2026-08-10
> **Stormchaser's Talent** is banned in Standard. In this deck it was: Backup engine — Otters off every spell.
> **Gran-Gran** is banned in Standard. In this deck it was: Cost reducer — each lesson in yard = -1 to instants/sorceries.
>
> Everything below is a record of how the deck played before the 2026-08-10 banned and restricted announcement. The list can't be registered as written, and the matchup numbers describe a field that no longer exists. Read it as history.
>
> Announcement: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/banned-and-restricted-august-10-2026


**Dominated the World Championship. 52–55% win rate against the field. Casters said: bring the deck you feel is favored based on skill edge, because this is just a good deck.**

Shares the Stormchaser Talent + Boomerang Basics shell with Izzet Prowess but adds the Monument to Endurance + Artist Talent combo as a second — and more powerful — engine.

---

## Game plan

Look like a slow control deck. Get Monument to Endurance and Artist Talent online together. Once that happens, draw your whole deck and kill with Monument drain triggers. The Otter backup plan closes games when the main combo gets disrupted.

---

## Play patterns

- **Gran-Gran** (Secrets of Strixhaven): reduces all instant and sorcery costs by 1 for each lesson in graveyard. Casters: "The missing piece of the puzzle — that cost reduction effect." This is the new engine piece that makes the deck tick even before Artist Talent levels up.
- **Monument to Endurance** fires drain triggers whenever you discard or cast a lesson
- **Artist Talent** (Strixhaven version) gives student discount on Accumulate Wisdom — essentially makes lessons free or near-free
- **Grand** (three lessons in graveyard) triggers Ancestral Recall mode — draw three. But with Gran-Gran active you don't even need this — "a very very big deal because you are gated by how much mana you have"
- **Three Steps Ahead** (Outlaws of Thunder Junction): copies a permanent on your battlefield. Copy Monument to Endurance = double drain output immediately.
- Double Monument + Artist Talent level 2: don't need Grand's three-lesson trigger anymore; each spell draws multiple cards
- Stormchaser's Talent as a backup engine — generates Otters when lessons are cast
- Boomerang Basics + Stormchaser Talent: buy back basics each turn, replay for more Otters
- Deck plays slowly early, then flips the game in a single turn once the engine is assembled
- Ray Jang went 12-1 at PT Secrets of Strixhaven and was first to lock top 8

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Fire Accumulate Wisdom in response to Soul-Guide Lantern**: the Ancestral still resolves before the graveyard gets exiled
- **Gran-Gran with 3 lessons in yard + 6 mana**: cast Consult for 1 mana → Monument triggers → make treasure → cast Combustion Technique free — entire sequence nets positive mana
- **Three Steps Ahead copying Monument**: immediately doubles all future drain output for free. Two Monuments on board = each lesson cast deals double damage.
- **Combustion Technique on opponent's upkeep**: sagas advance on first main phase, not upkeep — time this correctly to delay the saga chapter advancing
- **Double Monument + Artist Talent level 2**: once this is online the three-lesson trigger on Grand is irrelevant; you're just drawing your deck
- **Boomerang Basics bouncing your own Monument**: resets its three modes mid-turn — use a mode, bounce it, redeploy, use the same mode again
- **Monument drain scales with copies**: two Monuments out means double the drain on every discard/lesson

## Sideboard notes

**vs Boros Artifacts**:
- Bring in A Null (×2) — cleanest answer to Pinnacle Star Cage and Weapons Manufacturing
- Bring in Spell Snare (×2) — counters Weapons Manufacturing

**Watch for**: Rest in Peace from Boros Artifacts. It's an enchantment — Star Cage can't exile it. RiP permanently stops the lessons loop. Priority target.

**vs Mono Green Landfall**:
Watch for Soul-Guide Lantern (×3) from opponent. Fire Accumulate Wisdom in response to the activation — the Ancestral resolves before the yard gets exiled. Meltstrider's Resolve can hit Gran-Gran off the battlefield.

---

## Weaknesses

- Graveyard exile (Soul-Guide Lantern, Rest in Peace) disrupts the lessons loop
- Slow to assemble — vulnerable to fast aggro before Monument is online
- Drawing multiple Omnisciences (if running them) can brick hands
- Needs Artist Talent + Monument together; individually they're much weaker

## Variants — Spotlight Secrets London (2026-05-10)

Two distinct Lessons builds put up results outside the standard monument shell. Per OptimusTomTV (1UfUmY93K_k):

**Alexi Paula — Toth Lessons (Top 48 Spotlight, 61.5% win rate)**. Drops the Monument plan entirely. Toth fills a Young Pyromancer / Snapcaster Mage role: returns instants and sorceries from graveyard while making tokens. Pairs with the usual Stormchaser's Talent + Boomerang Basics shell. Sideboard adds **Origin of Metal Bending** to break opposing Monuments and the rest of the artifact/enchantment field (Cool But Rude, Talents). Roaring Furnace becomes a recurring threat — bounce it and recast off cards from Toth.

**Tamoya Kobayashi — Monumentless Izzet Lessons (Champions Cup JP/KR)**. No Monument. Uses **Ral, Crackling Wit** and **Aemiritus of Ideation** as engines instead. Roaring Furnace and Steaming Sauna play centerpiece. Long games turn into a Ral repeatable removal + card-advantage loop alongside Stormchaser's Talent piling Otters. Grand is still in the lessons package for late-game advantage. Wins by flipping a turn-4 Ral or ultimating into a wide Otter board.

## Weekend update — Champions Cup JP/KR + Spotlight Secrets London (2026-05-12)

Source: OptimusTomTV (1UfUmY93K_k). 65 pilots, 6.8% combined metagame share, 50.2% non-mirror win rate. Bounced back from a poor PT showing in a more open field.

**Helped this weekend**:
- vs Izzet Spellementals: 59% (graveyard tax + Sunderflock matter less when Lessons has minimal creatures)
- vs Jeskai Control: 55.2%
- vs Four-Color Control: 50/50
- vs Mono-Green Landfall: 64.7% (post-PT Mono-Green cut its Keen-Eyed Curator count to 2 main, 0 sideboard — less hate)
- vs Bant Rhythm: 76.2%
- vs Dimir Excruciator: favored

**Hurt this weekend**:
- vs Selesnya Landfall: losing
- vs Selesnya Ouroboroid: ~36% (Paw Patch Recruit replaces removed creatures with card draw; counters make burn ineffective)
- vs Izzet Prowess: depends on the version. Loses to "horse versions" — the ones built around Colorstorm Stallion that can clear Lessons' awkward ground blockers. Eddymurk Crab is hard to deal with outside of Combustion Technique.

The monument vs non-monument split is now strategic. Lessons pilots should pick a lane and commit.
