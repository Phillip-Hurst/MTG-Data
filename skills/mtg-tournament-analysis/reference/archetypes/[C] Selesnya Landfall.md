# Selesnya Landfall

> [!warning] Banned 2026-08-10
> This deck is built on the Mono Green Landfall core, which ran Badgermole Cub.
>
> Everything below is a record of how the deck played before the 2026-08-10 banned and restricted announcement. The list can't be registered as written, and the matchup numbers describe a field that no longer exists. Read it as history.
>
> Announcement: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/banned-and-restricted-august-10-2026


**Team Heavy Play's innovation — Mono Green base + white for Erode (4 main) and DiN Synthesis Amalgam. Chris Larson made top 8. Casters: "Built to prey on Izzet Prowess."**

Shares the full Mono Green Landfall core. White adds unconditional removal main and a post-board combo plan that Mono Green can't access.

---

## What white adds over Mono Green

- **Erode** (×4 main): destroy target creature or planeswalker, opponent gets a land. Unconditional. Hits animated lands. Games end too fast for the opponent's free land to matter.
- **Dyadrine, Synthesis Amalgam** (EOE): X+2 mana, 0/1 trample. Enters with +1/+1 counters equal to mana spent. Whenever you attack, remove a counter from each of two creatures you control — draw a card and create a 2/2 Robot token. Landfall constantly reloads counters, so every attack strips two of them for a card and a body.
- **Mossborn Hydra** (post-board): elemental subtype = **immune to Sunderflock**. Grows with landfall. Hard to remove once large. The post-board plan transforms into "almost a Mossborn Hydra combo deck."
- **Snakeskin Veil + Shelter by Ghost**: protect Mossborn Hydra from Erode in the mirror

---

## Play patterns

- Identical to Mono Green Landfall game one — see that file
- Post-board vs Prowess: Erode answers Slickshot Show-Off and other key threats cleanly; games end before the free land matters
- **Mirror (post-board)**: whoever assembles Mossborn Hydra + protection first usually wins. Larson ran full 4 of each protection spell; Stoyer ran 3 each. Larson won 2-1.
- Ba Sing Se + Fabled Passage = guaranteed extra landfall trigger every turn cycle for free

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Erode your own creature** if opponent tries to kill it —  triggers landfall, preserves board resources
- **Mossborn Hydra is an elemental**: Sunderflock bounces all opponent's creatures on ETB — Hydra stays. This is the primary post-board trump card vs Izzet Spell Elementals
- **Ba Sing Se + Fabled Passage**: Ba Sing Se animates the Passage, it attacks, then sacrifice it for two landfall triggers in one turn

---

## Mirror notes (Selesnya Landfall vs Selesnya Landfall)

Post-board revolves almost entirely around Mossborn Hydra. Erode cleans up the opponent's Hydra if you can resolve it cleanly. Snakeskin Veil + Shelter by Ghost protect your own.

Whoever stumbles on mana loses quickly. Both players have the same engine — execution and protection decisions decide it.

---

## Weaknesses

- Izzet Spell Elementals post-board: Sunderflock doesn't hit Mossborn Hydra but does bounce everything else. Need to resolve Hydra and protect it.
- Erode gives opponent a free land — in prolonged games (almost never) this could matter
- White mana requirements add marginal consistency risk vs Mono Green

## Weekend update — Champions Cup JP/KR + Spotlight Secrets London (2026-05-12)

Source: OptimusTomTV (1UfUmY93K_k). **102 pilots, 10.7% combined metagame share — most-played Landfall variant, second-most-played deck overall.** Combined 51.3% win rate.

The white splash continues to earn its slot: Erode answers threats unconditionally, Mossborn Hydra dodges Sunderflock, and the maindeck has more interaction than Mono-Green. Now a known quantity but still holding above 50%.

**Weekend matchup spread**:
- vs Izzet Prowess: 51.9% (slight edge to Prowess at 48.7% for Selesnya)
- vs Selesnya Ouroboroid: losing (62.5% for Ouroboroid in the snapshot dataset; faster clock + more interaction)
- vs Bant Rhythm: losing (Bant has Gear Hulk/Mering River tutor utility Landfall doesn't)
- vs Four-Color Control: 46.9% (n=32) — cub decks historically struggle into control; Bossing Say makes this less awful than Mono-Green's version
- vs Mono-Green Landfall: slight edge to Mono-Green in the head-to-head

Field is shifting to attack this deck. Pilots picking up the list cold against an experienced control opponent are likely to bounce off the 46.9% Four-Color matchup.
