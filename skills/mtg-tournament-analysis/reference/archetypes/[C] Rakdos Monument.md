# Rakdos Monument

**Casters: "Firmly in the other category — unknown deck that can pop up in top 16 or top 8." Runs Monument to Endurance as a drain and discard engine rather than as a combo piece.**

---

## Game plan

Get Monument to Endurance in play alongside Ini Iron Shield Elf. Equip Bloodthorn Flail. Discard to make Ini indestructible, trigger Monument drain repeatedly. Close with Flame Wake Phoenix or Moon Shadow scaling off creature deaths.

---

## Play patterns

- **Monument to Endurance + Ini Iron Shield Elf + Bloodthorn Flail**: re-equip flail to different creatures each turn; each discard triggers Monument drain
- **Ini Iron Shield Elf**: gains indestructible when you discard — like Adanto Vanguard but paying life vs. mana; hard to kill through blockers
- **Moon Shadow**: one-mana mythic from Lurin Eclipse — gets counters whenever creatures die; can become enormous
- **Flame Wake Phoenix**: flying haste; returns from graveyard when a 4+ power creature is in play — Ini qualifies
- **Marauding Mako**: play in tandem with Ini for recurring discard value
- **Bitter Triumph at instant speed**: triggers Monument on opponent's turn — drain on their clock

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Monument + Ini + Flail loop**: equip flail to Ini, discard to protect from removal, Monument drains 2; repeat every turn
- **Bitter Triumph at instant speed on opponent's turn**: triggers Monument drain without spending your own turn on it
- **Flame Wake Phoenix recursion**: Ini at 3+ power keeps the Phoenix coming back from graveyard every turn
- **Moon Shadow in combat**: opponent can't let creatures trade freely; each death grows Moon Shadow

## Sideboard notes

**vs Bant Airbending (boarding in)**:
- Death Mark — classic efficient removal vs Bant creatures
- Boulder Dash — kills Badgermole Cub and the land it animates; cast during upkeep so the animated land enters tapped
- Strategic Betrayal — possible bring-in vs creature-heavy boards

**vs Rakdos Aggro (aggressive decks run Monument too)**:
- The non-Rakdos decks use Wistfulness to destroy Monument
- Get Lost handles Ini Iron Shield Elf and other threats

---

## Weaknesses

- Wistfulness destroys Monument cleanly
- Reliant on Ini + Flail + Monument being in play together; disrupt any piece and the engine stalls
- Slower than straight Rakdos Aggro at closing games
- All aggressive decks run out of steam eventually — Monument mitigates this but doesn't fully solve it
