# Boros Artifacts

**Casters: "We should just call this Star Cage burn — that's what this deck is trying to do." Came to beat Badgermole Cub decks. Built around Pinnacle Star Cage as a damage engine.**

---

## Game plan

Get Pinnacle Star Cage into play. Feed it Ang's Iceberg (which generates mines and returns Star Cage) for a continuous loop. Each loop deals damage to the opponent. Bounce Star Cage with Nurturing Pixie or Sun Pearl Kieran to reset and redeploy. Win on damage from the Star Cage loop.

---

## Play patterns

- **Pinnacle Star Cage**: exiles creatures and artifacts the opponent controls; deals damage each time it fires
- **Ang's Iceberg**: cycle through the loop — play Iceberg, sacrifice to Star Cage, get new mines, return Star Cage with Iceberg's ability, repeat
- **Weapons Manufacturing**: produces mine tokens continuously — fuels Star Cage activations
- **Nurturing Pixie + Sun Pearl Kieran** (×4 each): bounce Star Cage to reset the loop; redeploy for more damage
- **Legion Extruder**: sacrifice mine tokens for additional value
- **Mevin Moxite**: rummage, produces munitions tokens
- **Ugan**: top-end closer once the loop has done enough damage
- **Soul-Guide Lantern** (main deck): graveyard disruption baked in

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Star Cage + Ang's Iceberg cycle**: Iceberg sacrifices to Star Cage for mines, Iceberg's ability brings Star Cage back — continuous loop as long as the pieces stay in play
- **Star Cage only exiles creatures and artifacts**: Rest in Peace is an enchantment — it does NOT get hit by Star Cage. Key for the Izzet Lessons matchup.
- **Nurturing Pixie bouncing Star Cage**: reset the loop, redeploy for more damage; also resets the exile trigger count for more pings
- **Weapons Manufacturing pumping out mines**: each mine is fodder for Star Cage activations and Legion Extruder sacrifices

---

## Sideboard notes

**vs Izzet Lessons (boarding in)**:
- Rest in Peace — key tech; survives Star Cage (enchantment, not creature/artifact); permanently stops the lessons graveyard loop

**Izzet Lessons boards in vs Boros Artifacts**:
- A Null (×2) — cleanest answer to Star Cage and Weapons Manufacturing
- Spell Snare (×2) — counters Weapons Manufacturing

---

## Weaknesses

- A Null and Spell Snare both disrupt the primary engine cleanly
- Rest in Peace is the deck's own tech but the opponent can also use it (if they have it)
- Came to beat Cub decks specifically — casters noted "no Cubs in sight" at Milwaukee
- Relies on multiple pieces staying in play; any single removal that hits Star Cage mid-loop is a tempo loss
