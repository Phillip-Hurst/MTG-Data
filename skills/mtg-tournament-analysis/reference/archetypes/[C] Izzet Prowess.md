# Izzet Prowess

> [!warning] Banned 2026-08-10
> **Stormchaser's Talent** is banned in Standard. In this deck it was: Primary engine — levels with every spell, makes Otters.
>
> Everything below is a record of how the deck played before the 2026-08-10 banned and restricted announcement. The list can't be registered as written, and the matchup numbers describe a field that no longer exists. Read it as history.
>
> Announcement: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/banned-and-restricted-august-10-2026


**Most-played deck at Portland RC (~16%) and PT Secrets of Strixhaven. ~50% Day 1 win rate at PT — the field came prepared. Average mana curve: 1.5.**

Can flex between aggro and control roles depending on the matchup. Casters: "Am I the aggro player or the control player?" — depends on what the opponent is doing.

---

## Game plan

Play cheap spells. Make a huge turn out of nowhere. Kill the opponent before they stabilize.

Three distinct kill vectors that all work off the same shell:
1. **Stormchaser Talent + Boomerang Basics loop** — recurring Otter tokens, infinite card advantage
2. **Eddymurk Crab tap lock** — bounce and replay to keep opponent's board tapped
3. **Colorstorm Stallion + Kicked Burst Lightning** — 11 damage off one spell

---

## Play patterns

- Open with cantrips (Slight of Hand, Flow State) to find the right threat
- Stormchaser's Talent levels up off every spell — generates an Otter token each time
- Buy back Boomerang Basics each turn: replay it off the Talent, generate another Otter, keep drawing
- Stormchaser's Talent + Get Out: level up Talent, pick up Get Out, pass — bounce two Talents and replay for infinite draw
- Eddymurk Crab at instant speed: two-mana 5/5 that taps all opponent's creatures. Bounce it with Boomerang Basics, replay the land, recast the Crab to re-tap everything
- Colorstorm Stallion: when five or more mana is spent on a spell, gets +1/+1 AND makes a copy of that spell. One kicked Burst Lightning = 4 + 3 (prowess) + 4 (copy) = 11 damage
- Traumatic Critique: three damage + loot. Acts like a fireball that replaces itself

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Stormchaser's Talent + Get Out loop**: level up Talent, pick up Get Out, replay — bounce Talents, repeat for infinite card draw
- **Crab bounce lock**: cast Eddymurk Crab, attacks tap their board, Boomerang Basics buys it back, replay the land, recast Crab on their upkeep
- **Stallion + kicked Burst Lightning**: 11 damage out of nowhere — 4 (bolt) + 3 (prowess trigger) + 4 (copy)
- **Traumatic Critique**: three damage upstairs + replaces itself — hits opponents in the air and keeps the hand full

---

## Sideboard notes

**vs Golgari Midrange**: opponent attacks on the graveyard axis. Watch for Strategic Betrayal (bounces Stallion, exiles graveyard) and Long Goodbye (kills Stallion without paying ward).

**vs Selesnya Landfall**: opponent boards in 4x Erode main (answers threats unconditionally) + post-board Mossborn Hydra (immune to Sunderflock). Edge Rover (×2) + Dragon Sniper (×1) is Stefan Schutz's specific tech for blocking Slickshot Show-Off.

**vs Mardu Discard**: bring in Slagstorm (×2) for board clear vs tokens and aggressive one-drops.

**vs Izzet Tempo (High Noon)**: hard matchup. One spell per turn kills the entire game plan. Izzet Tempo's creatures are all flash so they play around their own restriction.

---

## Weaknesses

- ~50% win rate at PT Secrets of Strixhaven — format had it figured out by Day 2
- Graveyard hate (Strategic Betrayal, Rest in Peace) shuts down the loop
- High Noon (Izzet Tempo) limits to one spell per turn — cripples the game plan completely
- Selesnya Landfall's Erode answers threats unconditionally; post-board Mossborn Hydra dodges Sunderflock
- Slagstorm / board wipes clear the Otter board cleanly
- Mardu Discard 2-0'd Mike Sigrist's PT-level Izzet Prowess build

## Weekend update — Champions Cup JP/KR + Spotlight Secrets London (2026-05-12)

Source: OptimusTomTV recap (1UfUmY93K_k). 219 pilots across both events, 22.9% combined metagame share — still the most-played deck.

**New tech**: builds are bringing **Into the Flood Maw** back into the maindeck or sideboard. Bounces a resolved High Noon, giving Prowess a way to save its combo turn against Azorius Flash. Against Flash specifically, the weekend matchup landed at 54-46 in Flash's favor over 63 games — not the lopsided result the lock piece would suggest on paper.

**Worst weekend matchup**: Izzet Spellementals at 44.6%. Spellementals has been favored vs Prowess for weeks now; no new tech is closing that gap.

**Slow against Ouroboroid**: Otter tokens block badly into pumped Aboroid creatures, especially when Paw Patch Recruit hands them trample. Past the first few turns, the burn package can't catch up to creatures that have been pumped twice.

**Overall weekend non-mirror win rate**: 51% across the field. Almost 400 mirror matches were played at the two events combined.
