# Azorius Momo

**Casters: "One of the best one-drops we've seen in a long time — does the same thing Llanowar Elf would do but also attacks pretty hard." Struggled vs Mono Green Landfall specifically; good against Prowess.**

Different from Azorius Flash (no High Noon). This build is centered on Quantum Riddler value and Momo Friendly Flyer as a ramp + pump engine.

---

## Game plan

Play Momo Friendly Flyer as an early ramp piece. Deploy flash flyers and Quantum Riddler to empty your hand for card draw. Close with a wide flying board augmented by Momo triggers.

---

## Play patterns

- **Momo Friendly Flyer**: taps to reduce cost of your next flyer; each flyer ETB pumps Momo. Casters called it "does the same thing Llanowar Elf would do but also attacks pretty hard."
- **Quantum Riddler**: refills hand each time you empty it. "You get paid off by trying to get all the cards out of your hand." Play as a warp for value; flash it in at end of turn.
- **Starfield Shepherd**: tutors land or key card; played at Warp cost for tempo
- **Cosmogrand Zenith**: creates soldier tokens (triggers Quantum Riddler / Momo); modular utility
- **Seam rip**: cheap removal for mana dorks (specifically for Llanowar Elves)
- **Ang Swift Savior**: flash flyer; "airbends" (taps) opponent's creature on ETB; can interact on opponent's turn even under High Noon if opponent runs it
- **Daydream**: flicker Quantum Riddler or Starfield Shepherd to reuse ETB triggers
- **Avatar's Wrath / Parting Gust / Disdainful Stroke**: interaction suite

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Warp Starfield Shepherd to fetch land, then warp Quantum Riddler**: draw cards when tapped out — generates value with minimal mana investment
- **Ang Swift Savior flashed in on opponent's turn**: doesn't trigger High Noon (which only applies during your turn); taps down a key attacker or creature
- **Daydream + Starfield Shepherd**: flicker the Shepherd on your opponent's end step to re-tutor a land on your turn
- **Cosmogram Zenith tokens + Momo**: each token ETB pumps Momo

---

## Good matchups

- Izzet Prowess — flash threats interact cleanly; Seamrip hits Llanowar Elves disrupting ramp
- General midrange and control

## Bad matchups

- Mono Green Landfall — Mossborn Hydra has no clean answer; Landfall goes too wide too fast
- Izzet Spell Elementals and Jeskai Control also noted as difficult

---

## Weaknesses

- No High Noon (unlike Azorius Flash) — no lock piece against spell-heavy decks
- Mono Green Landfall doesn't care about flyers the way Prowess does
- Quantum Riddler value is slow; needs to empty hand frequently to draw, which is awkward with countermagic held up
