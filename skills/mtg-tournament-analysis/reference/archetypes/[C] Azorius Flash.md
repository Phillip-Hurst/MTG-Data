# Azorius Flash

**Player quote (Zeven FA): "My deck tries to lock its opponent out of casting spells and finish the game off with cheap fliers."**

---

## Game plan

Resolve High Noon. Limit opponent to one spell per turn. Close with cheap flash fliers before they can recover.

---

## Play patterns

- **High Noon**: each player can only cast one spell per turn — cripples cantrip-heavy decks like Izzet Prowess that need to cast 5+ spells in a turn
- **Voice of Victory + AA Interrupter**: flash threats that come down while holding up High Noon mana
- Operate at instant speed, respond to opponent's one allowed spell, close the game with fliers
- Lean on cheap threats after High Noon lands — opponent can't use their one spell to remove the threat AND develop their own game plan

| Tashana's Tidebinder | Ability counter + flash body |

---

## Key tricks and interactions

- **High Noon vs Izzet Prowess**: Prowess needs to cast Flow State, Slight of Hand, Boomerang Basics, Burst Lightning, etc. in one turn to generate value. One spell per turn cripples all of it.
- **Tashana's Tidebinder countering Burnout Bastronaut ability**: Bastronaut can't pump to Speed 4, loses menace — kills the double-strike plan before combat
- **Ba Sing Se (Mono Green)**: activates as an ability, not a spell — High Noon is irrelevant. Mono Green doesn't cast spells anyway.

---

## Good matchups

- Any Slight of Hand / cantrip-heavy deck — one spell per turn makes the whole game plan fall apart
- Izzet Prowess — the single most punishing matchup for High Noon

## Bad matchups

- Mono Green Landfall — they don't cast spells; they just activate Ba Sing Se
- Decks with large individual spells that don't need volume (one Ouroboroid per turn is fine)

---

## Weaknesses

- High Noon hurts the pilot too — only one spell per turn limits the flash plan
- Narrow hate card: effective against spell-dense decks, irrelevant against ability/activation decks
- Small threats — cheap fliers may not close fast enough against decks that stabilize

---

## Weekend update — Champions Cup JP/KR + Spotlight Secrets London (2026-05-12)

Source: OptimusTomTV (1UfUmY93K_k). 42 pilots, 4.4% combined metagame share, **42.9% combined win rate — one of the worst performers among the popularity-tier decks**. Pilots not named Zeven Faust struggled to make it work in unknown hands.

**Where the deck still earns its slot**: **vs Izzet Prowess — 54% over 63 games**. This is a designated counter-meta deck and it does its job. If the field is going to be 23% Prowess, this is the deck for that field.

**Why it crashed elsewhere**:
- Prowess builds have added **Into the Flood Maw** maindeck or sideboard. Resolves a High Noon, Prowess bounces it on the combo turn, Flash can't reset tempo.
- Loses to both Jeskai Control and Four-Color Control (the deck has no game plan against permission backed by Day of Judgment).
- Loses to Selesnya Landfall (45.8% for Flash) — Cyborg Get Lost, Seam Rip on High Noon, Erode on threats.
- The eventual late game runs into 2-mana firefives, Anglers, and Interrupters that just outsize the cheap flier suite.

**The call**: bring it only when you're sure the room is Prowess-heavy. Otherwise it's a tier-D deck wearing a counter-meta jacket.
