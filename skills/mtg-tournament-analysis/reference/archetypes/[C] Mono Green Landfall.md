# Mono Green Landfall

> [!warning] Banned 2026-08-10
> **Badgermole Cub** is banned in Standard. In this deck it was: Mana acceleration (taps for 2).
>
> Everything below is a record of how the deck played before the 2026-08-10 banned and restricted announcement. The list can't be registered as written, and the matchup numbers describe a field that no longer exists. Read it as history.
>
> Announcement: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/banned-and-restricted-august-10-2026


**"The big bad of the format now." Best Day 1 win rates at PT Secrets of Strixhaven. Selesnya variant (Team Heavy Play) won the mirror by adding white.** Zero game-one interaction — all in on speed.

---

## Game plan

Play a large threat on turn one. Landfall triggers grow it. Attack with something very large very fast. No spells to speak of — just lands and creatures.

---

## Play patterns

- **Llanowar Elves** T1 → **Sazh's Chocobo** T2: the optimal curve. Chocobo grows with each landfall trigger.
- **Badgermole Cub**: taps for 2 mana; with 2+ in play, you can have 11+ mana by turns 3-4
- **Icetill Explorer** T3: gives two extra land drops that turn via mill trigger — can snowball Earthbender Ascension to 4 counters immediately
- **Earthbender Ascension** at 4 counters: all attackers get trample; landfall puts counters on creatures
- **Mightform Harmonizer**: doubles power of creatures with 2+ landfall triggers — can produce 32+ damage in a single attack
- **Ba Sing Se**: animates a land into a haste attacker AND triggers extra landfall. Use on Fabled Passage before cracking for repeated triggers.
- **Fetch lands** (Fabled Passage, Escape Tunnel): trigger landfall multiple times; Escape Tunnel returns from graveyard for a second use = 4 total landfall triggers in one turn
- **Sapling Nursery** (new): creates treefolk tokens with reach; goes wide fast. Good game one vs Prowess; vulnerable to Sunderflock post-board (bounces all tokens)
- Zero interaction game one — intentional. The deck is faster than most game plans if it draws well.

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Stack Earthbender Ascension trigger before Mightform Harmonizer**: creature goes to X from Ascension, then Harmonizer doubles to 2X. Wrong order = half the damage. This is the most important sequencing rule.
- **Harmonizer with Fabled Passage**: play Passage (trigger 1), crack Passage (trigger 2, gets a land = trigger 3) — Harmonizer has now doubled three times: 2→4→8→16 power
- **Sacrifice Escape Tunnel twice**: crack it once (landfall), it returns from graveyard, crack again = 4 total landfall triggers in one turn
- **Ba Sing Se on Fabled Passage before cracking**: animate it, attack (landfall), then sacrifice it = free extra landfall
- **Earthbender Ascension at 4 counters**: all attackers get trample — a board that couldn't push through suddenly does massive damage
- **Ba Sing Se doesn't cast a spell**: dodges High Noon, No More Lies, any "counter target spell" effect

## Sideboard notes

**vs Simic Rhythm**:
- Meltstrider's Resolve (×4) — exiles Cub and the animated land

**vs Izzet Lessons** (opponent boards in):
- Soul-Guide Lantern (×3) — exiles lessons from graveyard; kills Combustion Technique scaling and Gran-Gran cost reduction

**vs Izzet Prowess** (Stefan Schutz's tech):
- Edge Rover (×2) + Dragon Sniper (×1) — reach creatures specifically for blocking Slickshot Show-Off and other flyers

**White version (Selesnya)**: add Erode (×4) main + Mossborn Hydra post-board. See [C] Selesnya Landfall.md.

---

## Weaknesses

- Sunderflock (Izzet Spell Elementals) bounces the whole board at instant speed — game one loss if they develop an elemental
- Sapling Nursery tokens all get bounced by Sunderflock post-board
- Soul-Guide Lantern exiles the lessons loop but the real threat here is just that Izzet Lessons is faster at closing out the game
- Izzet Spell Elementals went 2-0 vs Mono Green Landfall with this as the decisive card

## Weekend update — Champions Cup JP/KR + Spotlight Secrets London (2026-05-12)

Source: OptimusTomTV (1UfUmY93K_k). Combined weekend overall win rate: 51.2%. Still doing well in an open field, but the matchup spread is shifting.

**Post-PT list change**: Spotlight London top 8 lists cut **Keen-Eyed Curator from 4-of main + sideboard down to 2 main, 0 sideboard**. The PT meta was full of graveyard-axis decks; the post-PT field isn't. Less Curator means less natural hate against Lessons and Spellementals — and the win rate against both fell because of it.

**Weekend matchups**:
- vs Izzet Prowess: 49.1% (basically 50/50)
- vs Izzet Lessons: 35.3% (the curator cut bites here)
- vs Izzet Spellementals: 43.8%
- vs Selesnya Landfall: slight edge for Mono-Green head-to-head
- vs Dimir Excruciator: 50% (n=10, theoretically favored)

If the field is going to be Lessons or Spellementals heavy, the Selesnya version is the better build — its maindeck interaction handles those matchups Mono-Green can't fight without Curator. If the field is open, Mono-Green Landfall is still a fine pick.
