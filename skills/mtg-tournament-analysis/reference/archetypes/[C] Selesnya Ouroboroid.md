# Selesnya Ouroboroid

**Matt Nass's build. Also called Selesnya Gearhulk. Casters: "Brightglass Gearhulk is really the important card." Won the featured match 2-0 over Izzet Spell Elementals.**

---

## Game plan

Ramp with Gene Pollenator + Llanowar Elves. Deploy Keen-Eyed Curator to eat opponent's graveyard. Use Brightglass Gearhulk to recur Maelstrom Wanderer's Resolve and answer large threats. Ouroboroid disperses counters when Pop Hatch is removed for reach damage.

---

## Play patterns

- **Keen-Eyed Curator** (×4 main): eats 2 spells from opponent's graveyard on each attack. Grows as it eats — becomes a 7/7 trampler with 4 different card types consumed. Against Izzet Spell Elementals this directly keeps Eddymurk Crab and Hearth Elemental offline by holding yard count below their discount threshold.
- **Brightglass Gearhulk**: fetches Maelstrom Wanderer's Resolve from deck or graveyard — use it to counter the opponent's Emrakul or other big threats. Matt Nass's primary interaction piece.
- **Pop Hatch with Offspring**: when removed, distributes counters to other creatures. Combined with Gene Pollenator + Llanowar Elves into Ouroboroid — 5-counter dispersal if Pop Hatch gets answered.
- No reach on most creatures ("these Selesnya decks do not have any reach") — all in on the ground game
- Ouroboroid is "win-more on top of the base" — the base (Curator + Gearhulk) does the work

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Track opponent's graveyard count**: with 3+ sorceries in yard and 6 mana available, Hearth Elemental deploys at discount — eat a card with Curator first to push the count below threshold
- **Brightglass Gearhulk on the crack-back**: let Eddymurk Crab tap everything, then cast Maelstrom Wanderer's Resolve on their tapped-out Eddymurk Crab (or after tapping triggers resolve)
- **Keen-Eyed Curator vs Rest in Peace**: Curator eats two cards per attack while RiP exiles everything. Opponent can't recur pieces.
- **Pop Hatch + Ouroboroid**: when Pop Hatch dies, counters spread to Ouroboroid and others — sudden large attacking threats

---

## Sideboard notes

**vs Izzet Spell Elementals (Selesnya boards in)**:
- Keen-Eyed Curator already in main — the deck is built around this matchup
- Rest in Peace — alongside Curator; also stops Brightglass Gearhulk recursion on their side

**Izzet Spell Elementals responds with**:
- Annul — counters Rest in Peace and Maelstrom Wanderer's Resolve (fetched by Gearhulk)
- Impractical Joke — 3 damage; kills Keen-Eyed Curator before it grows

---

## Weaknesses

- No reach — if opponent stabilizes the ground, the deck can't go over the top easily
- Sunderflock from Izzet Spell Elementals bounces the whole ground team at once
- Relies on Gearhulk staying in play; single removal on it cuts off the Resolve recursion
- Annul from opponent shuts down both Rest in Peace and the Gearhulk line simultaneously

## Weekend update — Champions Cup JP/KR + Spotlight Secrets London (2026-05-12)

Source: OptimusTomTV (1UfUmY93K_k). 41 pilots, 4.3% combined metagame share. **Top performer of the top-11 most-present decks: 54.6% non-mirror win rate.** Confirmed the PT Secrets reading that this is the best big-sample deck in the format.

**Beating the top of the field**: Prowess, Selesnya Landfall (the mirror to its no-rhythm-in-maindeck cousin), Izzet Lessons (64% — Paw Patch Recruit replaces removed creatures with card draw), Mono-Green Landfall (more velocity by skipping fetch-and-Ascension turns), Spellementals (50/50, which is a win against a deck that historically hammers cubs).

**Where it falls apart**:
- **vs Jeskai Control: 27.8% (n=18)**. Board wipes plus single-target removal on the Ouroboroid before the team gets pumped — the deck has no rebuild plan against repeated Day of Judgment.
- **vs Four-Color Control: 42.9% (n=7)**. Small sample but same dynamic — Inevitable Defeat exiles the enchantment threats, sweepers handle the bodies.
- **vs Dimir Excruciator with Deadly Cover-Up / Withering Curses + Requiting Hex**: black mass removal punishes the wide board even harder than blue counter magic.

The migration path is clear: Selesnya pilots who don't want the Landfall list's rhythm-less rebuild problem should be sliding here. Same color overlap, different finishers, more maindeck interaction against the Izzet field.
