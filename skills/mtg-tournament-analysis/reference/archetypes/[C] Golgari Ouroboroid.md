# Golgari Ouroboroid

> [!warning] Banned 2026-08-10
> **Badgermole Cub** is banned in Standard. In this deck it was: Ramp / mana acceleration.
>
> Everything below is a record of how the deck played before the 2026-08-10 banned and restricted announcement. The list can't be registered as written, and the matchup numbers describe a field that no longer exists. Read it as history.
>
> Announcement: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/banned-and-restricted-august-10-2026


**Black version of the Ouroboroid shell. More consistent than Simic — Lively Dirge provides tutors and card selection that Quantum Riddler can't match.**

---

## Game plan

Ramp with Badgermole Cub and Gene Pollinator. Slam Ouroboroid to put counters on everything. Attack with a pumped Deep-Cavern Bat (flying, lifelink, holds opponent's best spell) for a kill.

---

## Play patterns

- **Badgermole Cub** on turn 2 (off Lenoir Elves) — earthbends a land, makes it tap for 4
- **Gene Pollinator** triggers off every creature that enters — more ramp
- **Ouroboroid trigger**: when a creature with counters attacks, put two counters on each creature you control. Stack two Ouroboroids: first puts 2 counters on everything, second puts 3 — total 5 counters across the board
- **Deep-Cavern Bat**: took opponent's best spell on entry; with 5+ counters on it from Ouroboroid triggers it becomes a massive flying lifelinker
- **Lively Dirge**: tutor / card selection engine — effectively extra copies of Nature's Rhythm for finding Ouroboroid or Crater Hoof
- **Overlord of the Balemurk**: provides card selection and an additional permanent for Gene Pollinator to trigger off
- **Iridescent Vine Lasher**: early threat that scales with counters

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Sequencing two Ouroboroids**: attack with the first active; trigger puts 2 counters on everything; when second Ouroboroid's trigger fires it now sees larger creatures, so 3 counters — 5 total across the board
- **Deep-Cavern Bat + Ouroboroid counters**: after two triggers the Bat can be a 7/7+ flying lifelinker — kills in one hit and gains massive life
- **Ouroboroid + Deep-Cavern Bat holding opponent's spell**: opponent can't cast the spell they need to answer the board; you just attack with a huge lifelinker
- **Lively Dirge tutoring Nature's Rhythm**: redundancy for the draw engine; black version gets two ways to find key pieces vs Simic's one

---

## Sideboard notes

**vs Rakdos Aggro (boarding in)**:
- Requiting Hex — sweeper effect, buys breathing room
- Keen-Eyed Curator — better blocker early
- Predators — indestructible blocker

**Watch for Rakdos Aggro boarding in**:
- Twisted Fieldy — steals the Ouroboroid, puts roll on it, gives three counters to everything, then attacks
- Bitter Triumph — efficient answer to Ouroboroid
- Duress + Magebane Lizard replacing cheap removal

---

## Weaknesses

- Reliant on Ouroboroid staying in play to generate the pump
- Twisted Fieldy stealing the Ouroboroid is a blowout — suddenly their team is huge
- Slower than pure green versions in explosive turns
- Deep-Cavern Bat is the primary kill condition; if it dies the clock slows considerably
