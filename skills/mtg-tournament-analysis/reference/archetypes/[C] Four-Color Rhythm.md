# Four-Color Rhythm

> [!warning] Banned 2026-08-10
> **Badgermole Cub** is banned in Standard. In this deck it was: Core ramp engine.
>
> Everything below is a record of how the deck played before the 2026-08-10 banned and restricted announcement. The list can't be registered as written, and the matchup numbers describe a field that no longer exists. Read it as history.
>
> Announcement: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/banned-and-restricted-august-10-2026


**Winner of the Portland RC (Tristan Wild Laroo) played one Ouroboroid in the sideboard only. Casters: "Ouroboroid is overrated — mirror matches come down to Crater Hoof or burying with Quantum Riddler."**

Essentially the Simic Ouroboroid shell leaning harder on Nature's Rhythm for Crater Hoof wins and Quantum Riddler for card advantage, with additional colors for specific bullets.

---

## Game plan

Ramp with Badgermole Cub and Lenoir Elves. Tap Cub repeatedly to generate mana. Tutor Crater Hoof Behemoth with Nature's Rhythm for X (where X is big). Win with 11 creatures and massive trample damage.

---

## Play patterns

- **Badgermole Cub** turn 2 off Lenoir Elves — earthbends a land, makes it tap for 4 mana total
- **Badgermole Cub + Gene Pollinator**: each creature entering triggers Gene Pollinator; stack more creatures, spiral into larger and larger mana
- **Nature's Rhythm for X=8**: tap Lenoir Elf for 3, two remaining lands tap for 2, that's 10 mana — search Crater Hoof with 11 creatures on board = 44 trample damage
- **Quantum Riddler**: card advantage in the mid-game; grinds opponents down
- **Mockingbird**: copies a land or creature to accelerate mana. Copying a land = blue Rampant Growth
- **Formidable Speaker**: discard a land, find the best creature in the deck (Crater Hoof, Ouroboroid, whatever's needed)
- **Explosive Prodigy**: additional mana acceleration
- **Spider Manifestation**: can find and recur creatures from graveyard
- Brightglass Gearhulk + Mockingbird: value loop for specific interactive pieces

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Nature's Rhythm for X=8 with 11 creatures**: each creature gets +11/+11 trample — 44+ total damage in one attack
- **Mockingbird copying a land**: enters as a land tapping for whatever the copied land produces — effectively a ramp spell in green
- **Badgermole Cub earthbend**: tap the Cub, a land untaps and now taps for 4; with multiple Cubs and multiple lands this spirals extremely fast
- **Bant Airbending match**: Badgermole Cub can grow lands infinitely via Airbend loop if Bant uses Ang Swift Savior on Cub
- **Formidable Speaker in graveyard**: if the deck has recursion, Speaker can be replayed to find new pieces

---

## Weaknesses

- Meltstrider's Resolve exiles Badgermole Cub and the animated land — shuts down the ramp engine
- Weak to graveyard hate on Spider Manifestation / other recursive pieces
- If Crater Hoof gets answered before the attack resolves, board state may not be threatening enough
- Relies on assembling Cub + Gene Pollinator + Nature's Rhythm; hand disruption hurts
