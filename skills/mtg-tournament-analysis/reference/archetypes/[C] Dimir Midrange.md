# Dimir Midrange

**Feels like a blue control deck. Has 22 creatures on the list. Casters called game one the hard part — cards don't line up against every matchup, then you board them all out and bring the good stuff.**

---

## Game plan

Get on the board early with cheap flying threats, draw extra cards with Enduring Curiosity, and grind opponents out with Kaido Bane of Nightmares value. Removal package is tunable to matchup.

---

## Play patterns

- **Flitterwing Nuisance**: two-mana 2/2 (enters as 1/1 with -1/-1 counter); pay 3 to remove counter and draw a card when it deals damage. Lurin Eclipse addition.
- **Deep-Cavern Bat**: takes opponent's best spell, becomes a flying lifelink threat — grows with Ouroboroid buffs and draws off Enduring Curiosity
- **Enduring Curiosity**: enchant a lifelinker. Draw cards every time it attacks. Especially brutal on Deep-Cavern Bat (holds opponent's best spell, draws cards)
- **Kaido Bane of Nightmares**: surveil, attack, generate value — the main payoff card
- **Cecil Dark Knight**: transforms into a 4/4 lifelink paladin
- **Restless Reef**: creature-land that mills and draws when it connects
- **Bitter Bloom Bearer**: repeating token producer (Bitter Blossom reference)
- Removal package (Requiting Hex, Bitter Triumph, Shoot the Sheriff) — swap in postboard for matchup

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Enduring Curiosity on Deep-Cavern Bat**: holds opponent's best spell, draws cards every attack — disproportionate advantage when you're flying over their board
- **Kaido zero ability**: surveil and draw one — value mid-combat
- **Flitterwing Nuisance**: pay 3 on attack, draw a card — slow but reliable card advantage on a flying body
- **Shoot the Sheriff cannot target Hired Claw** (it's an outlaw) — important targeting restriction; need Bitter Triumph or Requiting Hex for the Lizard deck
- **Ouroboroid counters growing Deep-Cavern Bat**: the bat becomes a massive flying lifelinker; disproportionately impactful in Golgari Ouroboroid matchups

---

## Sideboard notes

**vs Mono Red Aggro**:
- Bring in Day of Black Sun — clears small creatures efficiently
- Bring in Vender Relentless — reasonable blocker that locks down aggro
- Bring in Spell Snare — punishes Slickshot Show-Off and other two-drops

---

## Weaknesses

- Game one is rough against matchups where removal lines up poorly
- Relies heavily on Enduring Curiosity staying in play — enchantment removal hits hard
- Shoot the Sheriff has outlaw restrictions (Hired Claw immune)
- Slower clock than aggro; needs early flyers to apply pressure
