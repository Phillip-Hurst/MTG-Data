# Simic Omniscience

**Caster comparison: "Like Splinter Twin. Turn four just kill you."**

When it goes off, the deck is unbeatable. When it doesn't, it can lose to itself.

---

## Game plan

Get Omniscience into play for free by stationing a planet off Kona Rescue Beasty's Survival ability. Once Omniscience is in play, cast everything for free. Win with Terror of the Peaks pinging the opponent through the Marang River Regent infinite loop.

Consistent turn-4 kill.

---

## Play patterns

- **Kona Rescue Beasty** has a Survival trigger: when a planet land is stationed off it, put Omniscience onto the battlefield (doesn't cast it — can't be countered)
- Use Evendo, Waking Haven or Uros Titanic Godcore as the planet land, tap Kona, trigger Survival
- **Omniscience in play**: cast Northwind Avatar for free, use it to wish for sideboard cards
- **Two Marang River Regents** bounce each other infinitely as long as any other permanent is in play — cast it over and over for free
- **Terror of the Peaks** is a dragon; each time a creature ETBs it pings the opponent. Loop two Merangs = infinite ETBs = infinite pings = dead opponent
- Formidable Speaker discards a card to find Kona or a combo piece
- Stockup and Consult the Star Charts dig for combo pieces quickly
- **Cavern of Souls** names Survivor or Dragon — protects Kona or Merang from counterspells

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Planet station doesn't cast Omniscience** — Get Lost can destroy it as an enchantment, but the Kona trigger itself can't be countered
- **Lost in the Maze at X=0**: free protection spell off Omniscience; mana value is 2, making it a Spell Snare target
- **Dispelling Exile countering a Spell Snare targeting Lost in the Maze**: layers of protection mid-loop
- **Merang + Formidable Speaker + any permanent**: bounce both Merangs repeatedly, cast Formidable Speaker infinitely, tutor Terror of the Peaks, ping to death
- **Two Merangs bounce themselves**: as long as one other permanent exists on the battlefield, the loop is infinite

## Weaknesses

- Can lose to itself — too many Omnisciences in hand bricks draws
- Needs multiple pieces; digs for a lot
- Get Lost destroys Omniscience as an enchantment (doesn't fight the Kona trigger but cleans up after)
- Torpor Orb (from Jeskai sideboard): Jiao had no answer; Marang River Regent can't bounce enchantments, locking out the loop entirely
- Hard counterspells on Kona stop the combo before it starts

## Temur Omniscience variant — Spotlight Secrets London (2026-05-12)

Source: OptimusTomTV (1UfUmY93K_k). 3 pilots across both weekend events, **60.6% combined win rate** (3rd place at Spotlight London). Same core engine as Simic Omniscience (Kona + Morang River Regent + Omniscience as 4-ofs), splashes red.

**Red adds**:
- **Ashling, Rekindle** — early-game advantage piece to get ahead before the combo lands.
- **Formidable Speaker** — combo-piece finder (already showing up in some other Kona lists).
- **North Wind Avatar** — 3-of in maindeck; gets sideboard combo pieces like a singleton Marang River Regent or an Omni Changeling that enters as a copy of the combo win condition.

Rest of the shell is familiar: Winter Knight Stories, Stock Up, Ill-Timed Explosion, Spider Sense, Spell Pierce.

The Temur build keeps the controlling Simic plan but adds early-game pressure and a more flexible toolbox via North Wind Avatar's tutor effect. Solid result against an open Spotlight field.
