# Izzet Tempo (High Noon)

**Built by Zean Fast specifically to prey on Izzet Prowess and Mono Green Landfall. Casters: "Very hard to play and very hard to sideboard with."**

Not the same as Azorius Flash — this is blue-red, uses High Noon with an entirely flash-based creature suite to get around the restriction.

---

## Game plan

Resolve High Noon. Restrict opponent to one spell per turn. Your creatures all have flash — play them on opponent's turns, staying within High Noon's rules while getting full access to your interaction. Close with flying damage.

---

## Play patterns

- **High Noon**: each player can only cast one spell per turn. Against Izzet Prowess (needs 5+ spells in a turn) and Landfall (spells matter for triggers), this is a complete lock.
- **All creatures have flash**: Ang Swift Savior, Floodpits Drowner, Voice of Victory — deploy them on opponent's turns where High Noon's restriction doesn't apply to you
- **Air Temple + Voice of Victory**: creates "a gigantic swing" attack combo; Voice prevents opponent from casting instants while you attack
- **Colorstorm Stallion + Boomerang Basics**: retained from the Izzet Prowess shell as a backup win condition
- The symmetry of High Noon hurts the pilot too — but since all your threats are flash, you're playing at instant speed anyway

## Key tricks and interactions

- **Flash creatures under High Noon**: opponent is locked to one spell per turn on your turn; you play your flash creatures on their turn where they're the one locked
- **Two flash creatures effectively = two spells**: one on your turn, one on theirs — opponent only gets one total
- **Voice of Victory + attack**: opponent can't cast instant-speed removal or combat tricks while you swing
- **Bounce High Noon if needed**: if the symmetry becomes a liability, Boomerang Basics can bounce it and you can counter it on the way back

---

## Weaknesses

- "Very hard to play" — managing the High Noon symmetry while maintaining tempo requires precise sequencing
- "Very hard to sideboard with" — narrow game plan with limited flexibility
- Loses to decks that don't cast many spells per turn (Mono Green with Ba Sing Se activations)
- High Noon is vulnerable to enchantment removal (Wistfulness, Get Lost)
